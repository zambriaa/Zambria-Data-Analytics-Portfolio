### 📊 Bank Customer Churn Analysis

SQL • Excel • Tableau

This project analyzes which customers are most likely to leave a bank. The dataset contains over 10,000 customer records, including demographics, account balances, and tenure.

The analysis focuses on identifying patterns across customer segments to understand where churn risk is highest.

**Key findings**

- Overall churn rate is **20.37%**, meaning about **1 in 5 customers left the bank**
- Customers aged **50 and older churn at around 45%**, more than five times higher than customers under 30
- High balance customers churn at **24%**, which is higher than low balance customers at **14%**
- Germany shows the highest churn rate at **32%**, compared to about **16–17% in France and Spain**

The Tableau dashboard summarizes these patterns and highlights the customer segments with the highest churn risk.

### 🏥 Healthcare Cost & Risk Analysis

SQL • Excel • Tableau

This project examines which factors contribute most to higher healthcare costs. The dataset includes demographic information, smoking status, BMI, and age.

The analysis focuses on identifying how these variables influence annual medical charges.

**Key findings**

- Smokers average **$32,050 per year in medical charges**, about **3.8x higher than non-smokers**
- Healthcare costs increase by **131% from the 18–29 age group to the 60+ group**
- Patients classified as **obese average about 49% higher costs** than those with a normal BMI
- When risk factors combine such as **smoking, obesity, and age over 60**, annual charges exceed **$40,000**

The Tableau dashboard highlights the main cost drivers and the highest risk segments.
